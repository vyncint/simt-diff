## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes.** Across fourteen independently constructed kernels, reconverge
0.1.6 did exactly what its documentation says — including the harder direction:
the three constructs it documents as warning-tier came back at warning tier
with no witness artifact, rather than being missed entirely or over-promoted.
The five safe-by-construction cases produced zero findings, which is
independent support for the "zero false positives at default confidence"
requirement, from kernels the project did not write.

**Does not establish.** This is one seed, one launch shape (`grid=1,
block=32`), one pin, one analyzer version, and fourteen hand-written
templates. It says nothing about constructs nobody thought to write. The
mutation engine and the launch matrix are what would widen it, and neither
exists yet.

**No bug was found.** That is the result, not a preamble to one.
